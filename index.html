THE THROUGH LINE

When the problem lives between functions, that’s where I work best.

Most enterprise problems are not contained neatly inside “marketing” or “creative.” They sit between strategy and execution, people and process, brand promise and operational reality.

I enter that ambiguity, learn the system, align the people, and build the infrastructure that lets better work happen repeatedly—not once.
