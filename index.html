How I connect the work

Same work.
A different lens.

Follow a creative request from brief to delivery. Each perspective reveals a different responsibility—and why the connections matter.

  1. 01 / Brief

    Find the purpose.

    Clarify the audience, the message and the reason for the work.

  2. 02 / Creative

    Give it expression.

    Translate direction into a recognizable, coherent brand experience.

  3. 03 / Review

    Protect the promise.

    Check that the communication is consistent, clear and appropriate.

  4. 04 / Delivery

    Make it hold together.

    Carry the identity and message consistently across the final touchpoints.

The brand perspective

A strong idea needs a consistent expression. Brand connects the intention in the brief to the experience people actually receive.

A little about me

I like the space
between disciplines.

John CotaLos Angeles, California

My background is in brand and creative. My work has increasingly been about what allows both to succeed.

That means connecting people who see a problem differently, bringing clarity to complicated decisions, and building practical ways for a team to move forward.

At L.A. Care, that work has spanned brand systems, creative operations, partnerships and marketing technology. Today, I’m also exploring how AI and automation can reduce friction while preserving human judgment.
