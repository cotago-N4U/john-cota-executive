Creative Services / Podio

Good work
needs a way
through.

A creative request can cross an entire organization. The system around it should help people move with clarity.

Role
Leadership of the brand and creative operation
Team
Creative, business partners, reviewers and vendors

Make the handoffs
part of the idea.

Creative Services managed Podio as a centralized workflow for intake, collaboration, regulatory review, approvals and production. It connected business partners, compliance, legal, translation, accessibility and vendors with creative delivery.

Standardized routing made ownership, revisions and documentation part of the work. Policies, forms and training materials made the model repeatable.

Follow the work

The same brief.
Three ways in.

Creative work has layers. Change the lens to see how brand, people and operations each shape the journey.

  1. 01 / Brief↗

    Find the purpose.

    Clarify the audience, the message and the reason for the work.

  2. 02 / Creative✳

    Give it expression.

    Translate direction into a recognizable, coherent brand experience.

  3. 03 / Review◉

    Protect the promise.

    Check that the communication is consistent, clear and appropriate.

  4. 04 / Delivery→

    Make it hold together.

    Carry the identity and message consistently across the final touchpoints.

The brand perspective

A strong idea needs a consistent expression. Brand connects the intention in the brief to the experience people actually receive.

I’m energized by the space between a good idea and making it happen.

My foundation is in brand, creative and marketing leadership. Over time, my work has grown to include the people, partnerships and operating models that allow all three to succeed.

At L.A. Care, that has meant connecting brand systems, creative operations, community partnerships and marketing technology. I enjoy bringing clarity to a complicated problem—and giving a team a practical way forward.

Photography remains part of how I see: finding the frame, noticing what others pass by and turning observation into a point of view.

Today, I’m also exploring how AI and automation can remove friction while keeping human judgment at the center.
